# Testing MOC

- [[Benchmarks]] — what the numbers mean, and which figures are real data versus extrapolation.
- [[Log Templates]] — daily, session, weekly.

## Why three times, not more

Testing costs a session and produces fatigue. Three points across 22 weeks is enough to see a trend and cheap enough not to interfere. Weekly max-hang testing turns training into testing and the noise swamps the signal.

## The rule that makes tests comparable

Same protocol, same edge, same time of day, same state of rest. A max hang measured Saturday after a Friday session isn't comparable to one measured on a rested Wednesday. Change anything and the number is a new baseline, not a comparison.
