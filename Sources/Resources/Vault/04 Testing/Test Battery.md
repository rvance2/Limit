# Test Battery

Run in [[Block 0 — Baseline Week]], again week 12, again end of week 22.

## 1. Finger strength — 7-second two-arm max hang

Lattice's protocol exactly, so my number is comparable to their 901-climber dataset.

- 20 mm edge (18 or 22 acceptable if that's what I own; record which).
- Half crimp, both arms, thumb off.
- Elbow 150-180° throughout. No lock-off.
- Warm up thoroughly, progressive hangs ~50% to 90%.
- Start moderate, add load each attempt, maximum within four to eight attempts.
- More than eight: stop, retry after 24 hours. Fatigue contaminates the number.
- Grip position change during the hang invalidates that attempt. Last clean attempt is the score.
- Record total load including bodyweight, kg and % bodyweight.

## 2. Pulling strength — 2RM weighted pull-up

- Shoulder-width pronated grip.
- Near-straight arms to chin over bar, controlled, no lower-body movement.
- Three minutes between attempts, adding load.
- Full range, bottom to chin over bar. No shoulder modification — version one carried one, based on an assumed anterior instability that doesn't match my presentation. Bars have never been a problem. [[Shoulder Capacity]].
- **Do not test a true 1RM.** Estimate it from the 2RM. General maximal-singles position, not a shoulder one.
- Record total load including bodyweight, kg and % bodyweight.
- Also record estimated 1RM from the 2RM. Don't test a true 1RM.

## 2b. One-arm lock-off hold time

The record of whether [[One Arm Progression]] is working.

- Three angles: full lock (chin at bar height), 90° elbow, 120° elbow.
- Each side separately. Both numbers matter; the difference between them matters more.
- Scapula set before the clock starts. A hold with a shrugged, protracted shoulder doesn't count.
- Assisted variants recorded as assisted, with the assistance method noted.
- Stop the clock at form breakdown, not failure.
- Record seconds per side per angle, and the asymmetry percentage.

Asymmetry over 15% is a stop-progressing signal, not a curiosity.

## 2c. Deep shoulder position capacity

The record of whether [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] is working.

- **Track A:** heaviest two-arm undercling hold at 30 s, and single-arm undercling hold time at bodyweight, each side.
- **Track B:** cross-body adduction hold time against a fixed band tension, each side, with and without the opposing arm loaded.
- Record the **click score 0-3** at the test load: 0 none, 1 faint, 2 obvious, 3 uncomfortable.

The result I want across 22 weeks is load going up while the click score stays flat or falls. Click score rising at a fixed load is a regression signal.

## 3. Critical force — with a load cell only

Fryer and Giles's all-out protocol:

- Half crimp, 20 mm rung, one hand at a time, into the force sensor.
- Rhythmic all-out MVCs: 7 s on, 3 s off.
- Four to five minutes, until output plateaus.
- Record critical force as mean end-test force in kg, plus the impulse above it.

Fatigue marker, not target. A drop of 8%+ from baseline means I'm accumulating fatigue faster than clearing it. [[Critical Force]].

## 4. Mobility

- **Box split.** Against a wall, toes and stomach touching, feet moving wider while contact is maintained. Upper-body support only for entry and exit. Record heel-to-heel in cm, and heel-to-heel divided by my height, because the raw number partly measures leg length.
- **Ankle dorsiflexion.** Knee-to-wall, both sides, toe-to-wall in cm.
- **Hip rotation.** 90/90 seated, both directions, degrees or a photo.

## 5. Shoulder screen

Run [[Shoulder Protocol]] at working load, not zero resistance. Note which items lack control and the click score 0-3 per item.

Also test both provocative positions deliberately: loaded undercling and cross-body isolation with the opposing arm. Record the load at which the click first appears. **That number rising over 22 weeks is the outcome I want** — it means the cuff is controlling more before translation starts.

## 6. Skill markers

Soft, and they matter.

- **Benchmark video.** Three boulders: flash level, solid limit, done-once. Side-on. Same three each test point, or as close as gym resetting allows.
- **Foot slips per session.** Counted across a whole session.
- **Attempts-to-send.** On the done-once benchmark, from cold.
- **Hip path.** From the side-on video, trace it. Total path length vs vertical gain. [[Movement Efficiency]].

## 7. Body composition

Bodyweight only. No skinfolds, no body-fat estimates. Anthropometric factors explain a tiny fraction of grade variance and tracking them invites the failure mode in [[Energy]].
