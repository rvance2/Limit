# Visualization Protocol

Two minutes, twice a day. Best-evidenced mental module and the cheapest thing in the plan.

## PETTLEP, applied

Holmes and Collins built it on functional equivalence — imagery should recruit as much of the same neural substrate as execution as possible. Seven elements, each an instruction:

| Element | What I do |
| --- | --- |
| **P**hysical | Stand up. Shoes on if convenient. Hands chalked. Hold the start position if I can. |
| **E**nvironment | At the boulder, or looking at a photo of it. Not the sofa. |
| **T**ask | The specific crux at my ability, not an idealised version. |
| **T**iming | Real time. A four-second move takes four seconds. Not slow motion. |
| **L**earning | Update as the skill changes. Yesterday's image of the crux is wrong today. |
| **E**motion | Include the nerves and the arousal. Sanitised imagery doesn't transfer. |
| **P**erspective | Internal, through my own eyes, primarily. External for checking body position. |

A systematic review of PETTLEP-based motor imagery found positive performance effects, larger at higher imagery frequency. Evidence also exists for strength gains and pain management from imagery built on the model.

## Daily

Morning and evening, two minutes each.

1. Stand. Eyes closed.
2. Real time, internal perspective.
3. Specific sensations: the crimp's edge on my finger pads, the exact angle of the foot, the moment of commitment.
4. Include the sensation of trying hard, and the nerves before pulling on.
5. If the image breaks or goes fuzzy at a specific move, that's the move I don't understand. Go look at the video.

## At the crag

One rehearsal in real time immediately before the attempt, in position under the boulder, hands chalked, reaching for the start holds without weighting them. Then go.

One. Not five. Five becomes rumination and pushes [[Arousal]] the wrong way.

## The skin case

Best argument for this module. A split takes a week, muscles recover in two days. On day three of a four-day trip, imagery is practice with zero skin cost and worth more than another low-quality attempt. [[Skin]] and [[Patience]].

## What it won't do

Build force. It sharpens execution of movements I can nearly do. It won't make an unholdable crimp holdable.
