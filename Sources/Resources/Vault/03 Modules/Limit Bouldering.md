# Limit Bouldering

The centre of gravity of this plan. Boulders with one or two moves right at my limit, worked as moves rather than boulders.

## What it isn't

Trying hard boulders. Hampton's framing: by definition I need to be at my limit, so I won't top out, and the mindset that requires sending every try is what stops people doing this correctly. He's direct — some of his most productive training seasons involved not sending a single hard boulder in the gym.

If I send it, it wasn't a limit boulder. Set a harder one.

## Dose

- 2-3 problems, each with one or two moves at my limit.
- 6-10 total genuine attempts.
- 3-5 min rest between attempts. Timer, not feel. PCr resynthesis is functionally complete at 3-5 min; at 60 s I'm at 85%, and an 85% attempt is wasted.
- 45-75 min. Longer means I'm not resting enough or doing too many problems.
- Twice weekly, Blocks 2 and 3.

## Setting them without a systems board

Gym boulders and possibly a spray wall. Hampton's guidance is manipulating variables one at a time. In a commercial gym:

- Take a boulder two grades above my limit and delete the topout. Work moves 3-5 only.
- Take one at my limit and remove a foothold, or an intermediate.
- Link the crux of one boulder into the crux of an adjacent one.
- Use the steepest wall available. Steep makes moves harder through force and tension rather than fingertip precision on tiny gym crimps — the kind of hard I want, and it spares skin.
- Sit starts. Cheapest difficulty multiplier in any gym.

## Simulation sets

Once a project is chosen, two of the session's problems should come from [[Simulation Sets]] rather than being picked off the wall. Same attempt budget, same rules — the sims don't get extra attempts.

## Structure

1. Full warm-up, 20 min, ending with two boulders 3-4 grades below limit.
2. One near-limit boulder as potentiation, if it's a good day.
3. Problem A: 3-4 attempts, 4 min rests.
4. Problem B: 3-4 attempts, 4 min rests.
5. Optional C if attempt quality is holding.
6. Stop.

## Stop rules

- Three consecutive attempts worse than the session best. Over. [[Attempt Discipline]].
- Any fingertip hot spot. Over. Skin costs a week and the remaining attempts are low quality anyway.
- Shoulder **pain** on a move: abandon that beta. A painless click in a thuggy undercling is not a stop — log it on the 0-3 scale from [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] and keep going. Those are the moves I want to make easier.
- Never after [[Recruitment Pulls]] at full intensity, never after [[Max Hangs]] to a small margin.

## Logging

Every attempt: number, highest hold, genuine or rehearsal, one clause on the failure mode. This is the data that makes [[Beta Lab]] work.
