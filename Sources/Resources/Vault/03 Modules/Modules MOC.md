## Notation

Eva López notation throughout. `3 x MAW Hangs x 20mm x 10" (3) :3'` = three sets, added weight, 20 mm edge, ten-second hangs, three-second margin before failure, three minutes rest. The margin is the important part. [[Max Hangs]].
