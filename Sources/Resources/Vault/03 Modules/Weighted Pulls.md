# Weighted Pulls

Base strength driver for [[Pulling Strength]] and the load base under [[One Arm Progression]].

## Dose

| Block | Prescription |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 × 5 @ 75-80% est. 1RM |
| 2 | 5 × 3 @ 85-90%, one top double at 92% in weeks 10-12 |
| 3 | 4 × 4 @ 82-85%, maintenance while [[One Arm Progression]] takes the load |
| 4 | 3 × 3 @ 80%, maintenance |

3 minutes rest. Full range — bottom to chin over bar.

Pair with rows, 3 × 8-10, for balanced scapular work. Not as a restriction, just because horizontal pulling builds the pivoters and I want them.

## What changed from version one

Version one capped top sets at 90%, banned singles, forced a 2:1 row-to-pull-up ratio, and required stopping 5-10° short of full extension at the bottom. All of that came from an assumed anterior instability. Bars have never been a problem for me — 14 pull-ups on an average day, 23 on a good one, clean muscle-up — so the bottom-of-range restriction in particular was solving a problem I don't have.

Removed. Full range, load it properly. [[Shoulder Capacity]] has the correction.

The one thing kept: no true 1RM testing. Estimate it from a 2RM or a 3RM instead. That's a general "maximal singles have poor return-to-risk for a climber" position, not a shoulder position.

## Progression

+2.5 kg when all sets hit the top of the rep range with clean form. Recalculate estimated 1RM every four weeks from a 2-3RM rather than testing a single.

## Where I probably am

14-23 bodyweight pull-ups plus a clean muscle-up suggests general pull strength is not my limiter. [[Benchmarks]] puts the threshold around a 2RM with 30-40% bodyweight added, and I may already be past it — high-rep performance and max strength diverge, so test rather than assume in [[Block 0 — Baseline Week]].

If the test comes back above the threshold, this module is serving [[One Arm Progression]] rather than serving my grade, and that's fine as long as I know which.

## The OAPU gate

Community heuristic: a strict one-arm pull-up needs a weighted pull-up 1RM around bodyweight + 50-65%. No research behind it, useful as a direction. If my 1RM is well under +50% BW, more of this module and less rushing the ladder.

## Stop rules

- Pain, as distinct from muscular fatigue. Not the painless click, which doesn't appear on bars.
- Medial elbow tenderness: cut load 20%, double [[Antagonist and Wrist]].
- Dead-arm or sudden weakness at any point: stop, get assessed.
