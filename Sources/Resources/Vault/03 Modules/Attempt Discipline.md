# Attempt Discipline

Rules for a project session. The highest-return module in the vault for me, and entirely unglamorous.

## Four rules

**1. Timer between attempts.** Three minutes minimum, five on the crux. Not "when I feel ready" — I feel ready at 90 seconds and I'm at ~85% of capacity. PCr resynthesis is largely complete at 1-2 min and functionally complete at 3-5. An 85% attempt on a limit boulder is wasted and it costs skin.

**1b. A second reason for the timer.** Holds warm up when they get rapid-fired — a hold attempted repeatedly stays warm and greasy. So the rest interval is protecting friction as well as phosphocreatine. Two independent reasons for the same three to five minutes. [[Friction]].

**2. Fixed attempt budget, decided before the session.** Six to ten genuine attempts. Written down before I start. Deciding during means deciding while frustrated.

**3. Stop after three consecutive attempts worse than my best of the day.** Performance decline means the useful part is over regardless of remaining daylight. This will feel wrong every single time and it's right.

**4. Log everything.** One line per attempt. Number, highest point, genuine or rehearsal, one clause on the failure mode.

## Log format

```
1 | rehearsal | worked moves 1-3
2 | genuine   | crux hold, popped — foot slipped
3 | genuine   | crux hold, held it, fell reaching lip
4 | genuine   | fell before crux — arrived with hips wrong
5 | not genuine| tired, didn't commit
STOP — rule 3
Skin: 3/5. Rock 6°C, dew point 1°C, light wind.
```

Ten seconds per attempt, and the difference between twelve sessions of data and twelve sessions of vibes.

## Naming the attempt in advance

Out loud or silently: "this is a real one" or "this is a rehearsal." Rehearsals are useful and allowed. Attempts that were secretly rehearsals aren't, because they contaminate my data about whether the move is possible. [[Intent]].

## Skin as currency

A split takes a week, muscles recover in two days. On sharp rock my attempt count is skin-limited before it's strength-limited, so every attempt is a purchase. Rules 1 and 3 are partly skin management. [[Skin]].

## Block-level

Once I've chosen a sequence in [[Beta Lab]], three sessions before re-opening the question. Lattice's finger rule — hold a method 6-12 weeks before assessing — applies to beta and to training methods equally.

Method-hopping and beta-hopping feel like progress. They're the main way I'd train for years without changing.
