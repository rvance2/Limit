# Grip Specificity Block

Narrow, late-phase grip work. Only makes sense with twelve weeks of half crimp behind it.

## Rationale

Lattice prescribe half crimp in 98% of logged fingerboard sessions and argue specificity comes later, in the peak phase, targeted to the individual's project. Their assessment data shows the strongest climbers converge to nearly identical loads across grip types (+55.5 kg half crimp vs +55.3 kg open hand at V12+), having built the broad base first.

Whether that convergence is caused by half-crimp training or merely correlates with being strong isn't established. Either way the sequencing is defensible: broad first, narrow last.

## Dose

- 2-3 sets per position, 7-10 s, 3 min rest.
- Two positions maximum per session.
- Once weekly in [[Block 2 — Max Strength]] and [[Block 3 — Power and RFD]], twice in [[Block 4 — Peak and Send]].
- Selected by the project's actual demands, not a checklist.

## Selection

| Crux hold | Train |
| --- | --- |
| Sharp incut crimp | Full crimp, yielding sets only, 20 mm |
| Two-finger pocket | Front two and back two, matched volume |
| Sloper | Open hand at large joint angles, on an actual sloper |
| Pinch | Pinch block, thumb loaded, both widths |
| Undercling or gaston | Half crimp with the shoulder in the project position |

## Full crimp caution

Full crimp raises flexor pulley forces. Rules if I use it:

- Yielding sets only. No overcoming isometrics in full crimp.
- Before climbing, never after.
- 20 mm edge, which is about right for the position.
- Two sets, not five.
- First sign of DIP or pulley discomfort and it's gone for the block.

## Moved earlier

Version one held this until Block 3. Moved into Block 2 because breadth is a base-phase property, not a peaking one, and because Block 3 now belongs to rate work.

Lattice's convergence data is the argument: at V1-V4 open-hand testers loaded +22.5 kg against half crimp's +14.5 kg, and by V12+ the two are within 0.2 kg. The strongest climbers have no weak positions. At V8-V10 that's a gap worth closing now rather than during a peak phase. [[Factors MOC]].

Still not in Block 1. Specificity before any base at all is how I'd accumulate pulley injuries while feeling clever — five weeks of half crimp first.
