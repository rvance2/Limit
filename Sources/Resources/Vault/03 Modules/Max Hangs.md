# Max Hangs

Added-weight maximal dead hangs on a fixed edge. Base and peak finger module. López calls it MAW Hangs.

## Dose

`3-5 x MAW Hangs x 18-20mm x 7"-12" (2-4) :3'-5'`

- 3-5 sets, build to 5 by week 4 of a block.
- 18 or 20 mm, half crimp, thumb off.
- 7-12 s per hang.
- Margin 2-4 s. Could hold 14, hang 10.
- 3-5 min rest. Take all of it — neural work.

## The margin is the method

López, via González-Badillo's *carácter del esfuerzo*: leaving a buffer gives comparable strength gains to training to failure, with less overuse risk and faster recovery. Her cites — Davies on repetition failure, Morán-Navarro on recovery time course, Sampson and Groeller on whether failure is necessary. Answer across all three: it isn't.

Second reason: intensity control. Go to failure every set and fatigue forces the load down set by set. The signature shifts glycolytic and phosphagen-depleting, and I trade the neural adaptation for an endurance one.

I'll be bad at estimating the margin at first. Accuracy comes with reps. The estimating is the price of the right adaptation.

## Warm-up

3-4 progressive hangs if I've already climbed, 6-8 if not, 50% → 90% of the day's load. Last warm-up set tells me set one's load.

## Progression

- +1-2.5 kg when I hit the prescribed duration with the prescribed margin twice in a row.
- Ceiling 5% added load per session. [[Tendon Health]].
- Twice weekly. Three times only in [[Block 2 — Max Strength]] and only with no morning finger stiffness.
- Hold the method 6-12 weeks before judging it.

## Stop rules

- Grip position change mid-hang ends the set; it doesn't count.
- Palpation tenderness over a pulley: switch to 25-30 mm, drop load 30%.
- Morning stiffness taking over 15 min to clear: reduce volume 30% for a week.
- Never at the end of a session.

## Setup

Edge width ~50 cm so hands sit at elbow width or slightly wider. Narrower forces pronation and overloads pronator teres, wrist, elbow. Shoulders engaged, not passively hanging — doubly true for me. [[Shoulder Protocol]].
