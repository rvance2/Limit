# Deep Shoulder Positions

Graded loading of the two positions that currently click: thuggy underclings, and fully cross-body isolations with the other arm pulling opposite. Goal is that both become weapons rather than symptoms.

This is the module for "I'd like deep shouldery moves to be easier." It's also the treatment, if the mechanism guess in [[Shoulder Capacity]] is right — the cuff can't control what it's never asked to control.

## Principle

Exposure, graded. Not avoidance. Load the position at an intensity where the click is absent or minimal, hold there until it's comfortable, then add load. If a position clicks at 20 kg and is silent at 12 kg, train at 12 and work up.

## Track A — underclings

| Stage | Exercise | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Two-arm undercling hold on a bar or jug, feet on | 3 × 30 s |
| 2 | Two-arm undercling, feet off, hips driven up into it | 3 × 20 s |
| 3 | Single-arm undercling hold, feet assisting | 3 × 15 s each |
| 4 | Single-arm undercling, feet off | 3 × 10 s each |
| 5 | Undercling-to-move: pull up into the undercling and reach with the free hand | 3 × 5 each |
| 6 | Thuggy undercling sequences on the wall, deliberately sought out | in [[Limit Bouldering]] |

The undercling loads flexion plus external rotation with the lats firing hard, which is the position where the head wants to translate superiorly and anteriorly. Stage progression is about the cuff catching up with that vector.

## Track B — cross-body isolations

| Stage | Exercise | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cross-body adduction hold against a band, static | 3 × 20 s each |
| 2 | Same, with the opposite arm pulling the other direction | 3 × 20 s each |
| 3 | Half-kneeling anti-rotation press (Pallof) at end range | 3 × 10 each |
| 4 | Cross-body reach from a locked-off position, weighted vest | 3 × 6 each |
| 5 | Cross-through moves on steep wall, deliberately sought | in [[Style Roulette]] |

Track B is a rotational control problem more than a strength one. The opposing-arm version in stage 2 is the specific thing that provokes it, which makes it the specific thing to build.

## Dose

Two tracks, alternating sessions, ~15 min each. Both in [[S6 Skill and Pull]] and Monday recovery.

Advance a track when the target is hit for two consecutive sessions with no more than a faint click and no pain.

## Stop rules

- **Pain.** Not the click — pain. Stop, and get the assessment.
- Dead-arm or sudden weakness. Stop everything.
- Click getting louder or appearing at lower loads over consecutive weeks. Regress a stage, and if it doesn't settle in two weeks, get assessed.
- The click staying the same while load goes up is the outcome I want. Log it.

## Logging

Per session: track, stage, load, hold time, and click on a 0-3 scale (0 none, 1 faint, 2 obvious, 3 uncomfortable). The interesting number over 22 weeks is load going up while the click number stays flat or falls.
