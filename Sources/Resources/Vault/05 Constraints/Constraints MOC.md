# Constraints MOC

Four things about my body override the plan rather than fitting inside it. Constraint and block prescription conflict → the constraint wins.

- [[Shoulder Capacity]] — painless positional clicking in two loaded positions. A capacity gap to train, not a restriction to program around. [[Shoulder Protocol]] and [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] are the response.
- [[Lower Limb Resilience]] — occasional severe shin splints, triggered by approach volume spikes.
- [[Skin Programme]] — sweaty skin on Antihydral. Governs attempt counts.
- [[Readiness Monitoring]] — the daily override mechanism.

## The restriction list, in one place

Pulled from the module notes so I can see it all at once.

**Shoulder**

Bars and edges have never produced symptoms; the click appears only in loaded underclings and cross-body isolations, is painless, and leaves nothing behind. [[Shoulder Capacity]] has the reasoning.

What actually remains:

- **Load the provocative positions, don't avoid them.** [[Deep Shoulder Positions]], graded by load, twice weekly.
- [[Shoulder Protocol]] 3×/week for all 22 weeks including reduced weeks. Now a strength module, not a band routine. Last thing cut.
- No true 1RM testing on [[Weighted Pulls]]. Estimate from a 2-3RM. General maximal-singles position, not a shoulder one.
- Book an assessment. Not a gate on anything.

Removed: bottom-of-range pull-up limit, 2:1 row ratio, 90% load cap, full-dyno exclusion, one-arm catch exclusion, [[Contact Strength Ladder]] stage 7 exclusion, [[Deadpoint Power]] feet-on requirement, shallow dips, and the six-week gate on [[One Arm Progression]].

**Shins**

- Approach volume up no more than 20-30% per week.
- Two weighted walks per week starting two weeks before any trip with real approaches.
- Multi-day trips: alternate long-approach days with roadside days.
- [[Lower Limb Resilience]] twice weekly, including the bent-knee heel raises everyone skips.

**Skin**

- Antihydral off the finger creases.
- Moisturise on Antihydral days. The step that prevents splits.
- File after sessions, not before, and 24-48 h before a project day.
- Attempt budget is partly a skin budget. [[Attempt Discipline]].

## Get it assessed

Painless clicking is common and often benign, and I'm not qualified to know which kind mine is. Book it. But it gates nothing — there's no case for pausing training over a painless, positional, functionally silent sensation in a shoulder that does 23 pull-ups.

## Red flags — stop and get assessed

**Escalate immediately if any of these appear.** The painless click on its own is not one of them.

- **Pain**, in the position or afterwards. The current sensation is painless; pain is a change.
- A **dead-arm** or sudden-weakness sensation.
- A discrete event where the joint goes and comes back — a jolt, then aching for hours or days.
- Symptoms appearing in **bar or edge work**, which has always been clean. That's a pattern change.
- Numbness or tingling down the arm, or night pain.
- Any finger pop, click, or sudden sting.
- Focal bone pain on the tibia, as distinct from diffuse ache along it.
- Pain gripping a doorknob or a mug — elbow tendinopathy, and it takes months if I train through it.
- Medial elbow tenderness after one-arm work. Second most likely injury in [[One Arm Progression]] after the shoulder.
