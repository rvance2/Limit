# Environment MOC

Can't train the weather. Can forecast it, plan around it, and stop attributing its effects to my fitness.

Honest weighting: a small share of variance across a season, a very large share on any given day. A boulder that goes in November at 4°C on fresh skin doesn't go in July at 26°C on day three of a trip, and no amount of training closes that gap.
