# Rock

Surface roughness, grain, and how they change what my skin and rubber can do.

## What the research says

Classical rock mechanics found that at the pressures studied, friction between rock surfaces is largely independent of rock type and initial surface roughness. That's rock on rock and doesn't transfer to skin on rock, a completely different tribological system.

For skin on rock, Clarke et al. (2026): finger pad contact and chalk effectiveness on sandstone, granite, dark and light limestone with varying roughness. The finding that matters — chalk's effectiveness depends on moisture level and on the gradient of the asperities. Roughness isn't a single scalar predicting grip; the *shape* of the roughness matters.

## Characteristics

**Sandstone.** Rough, soft, erodible. High friction on skin and rubber, eats skin faster than anything. Weakens wet, which is where the 24-48 hour rule after rain comes from. Grit and desert sandstone are the extremes — exceptional friction, brutal on skin.

**Granite.** Smooth surface, crystalline texture. Friction comes from crystals rather than surface roughness, so it rewards precise placement over smeared contact area. Hard on rubber edges.

**Limestone.** Variable. Can be rougher than sandstone, can be polished to glass by traffic. Polished limestone is where soft rubber and minimal chalk win.

**Gritstone.** Coarse, rounded, friction-dependent, famously condition-sensitive. Skin-destroying.

## What follows

Can't change the rock. Three consequences:

1. **Skin budget.** On sandstone or grit my attempt count is skin-limited before it's strength-limited. Fewer, better attempts. [[Patience]].
2. **Rubber choice.** Harder for abrasive, softer for polished. [[Rubber]].
3. **Brushing.** On polished or high-traffic rock, brushing is worth more than anything else I'll do that day. Old chalk is a lubricant. [[Friction]].

## Training implication

Gym holds are polyurethane and behave nothing like rock. A crimp that's fine on plastic can be unusable on grit and vice versa. If the project is on a specific rock type, get on that rock type in the last four weeks. One reason [[Block 4 — Peak and Send]] moves volume outdoors.
