# Physical MOC

## Force
- [[Finger Strength]] — best physical predictor at every level tested.
- [[Grip Positions]] — same tissue, different force at different joint angles, different training response.
- [[Pulling Strength]] — second best, decays faster. Also where my one-arm goals live.
- [[Shoulder Capacity]] — a capacity gap in two specific loaded positions. Trained by [[Shoulder Protocol]] and [[Deep Shoulder Positions]], not programmed around.

## Rate
- [[Rate of Force Development]] — how fast the force arrives. Separates the top end more cleanly than peak force.
- [[Contact Strength]] — the applied version: catch and stabilise inside ~200 ms.
- [[Recruitment]] — the neural side.

## Transmission
- [[Tension]] — whether force from the feet reaches the fingers.
- [[Leverage]] — the geometry deciding how much force reaches the hold.
- [[Power]] — force × velocity. What limit boulders actually demand.

## Tissue and range
- [[Critical Force]] — fatigue threshold. Near-irrelevant to my goal, and the data says so loudly.
- [[Flexibility and Mobility]] — barely predictive of grade, occasionally decisive for one move.
- [[Tendon Health]] — the ceiling on how fast I add load.
