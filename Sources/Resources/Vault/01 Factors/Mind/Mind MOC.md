# Mind MOC

At V13+ finger strength explains 4% of grade variance and pulling strength explains nothing. Something else does the work. Some skill, some tactics, some this.

Careful here. This domain attracts more confident nonsense than any other part of climbing training, and the good research is narrower than the popular accounts.

- [[Arousal]] — the activation level where I perform. Individual, measurable, manipulable.
- [[Focus]] — where attention goes, and what happens when it goes elsewhere.
- [[Intent]] — the difference between trying and trying hard. Bigger than I expect.
- [[Commitment]] — going when going is the only thing that works.
- [[Patience]] — project-session discipline. My most likely failure mode.
- [[Visualization]] — the one mental skill with a decent evidence base and a protocol. Implemented by [[Visualization Protocol]].

## My ranking

[[Patience]], then [[Intent]], then [[Arousal]], then the rest. Attempt discipline is a bigger lever than any amount of self-talk work, and it's boring, which is why it stays untrained.
