# Precision

Landing where I intended, first time, hands and feet. Compounds with everything — an imprecise hand catch demands more [[Contact Strength]], an imprecise foot demands more [[Tension]], both cost skin and time.

## Why it matters more on a limit boulder

On a V4 I have margin. Two centimetres off the sweet spot on a jug and nothing happens. On a limit boulder the sweet spot may be the only spot, and the crimp's best edge versus one centimetre left of it can be the whole problem.

Second-order effect worth naming: imprecision produces variance, and variance means I can't tell whether an attempt failed because the move is too hard or because I hit the hold badly. That makes it impossible to know whether to train or change beta. Precision is partly an information-gathering tool.

## Measurement

Count. Per attempt on a project: did the hand land on target, yes or no. Same for the crux foot. Ten attempts gives a hit rate. Under 70% on the crux hold means the move isn't my limiter — accuracy is — and more maximum-effort attempts won't fix it.

## Training

- [[Footwork Precision]] handles feet.
- Hands: the slow-motion catch. Crux move at 60% speed, stopping the hand a centimetre short, then placing it. Ten reps. Then 80%. Then full speed. Closer to how gymnasts train landings than how climbers train moves.
- Eyes on target until contact, hands as well as feet. I look away from a hold before my hand arrives.

## One tick mark, not two

Standard practice is a goalpost tick — one mark for the index edge, one for the pinky. An elite climber working a V17 was doing exactly that and landing consistently two inches off a hold with no margin. His coach's fix: aiming at one thing is more accurate than aiming at two. Accuracy stopped being the problem that session.

Free, obvious in hindsight, and he said it never entered his field of view because there was too much else to think about on a hard attempt. [[Burden of Dreams — Wheeler and Shriber]].

Test it on my next project session. Single tick, centred on the part of the hold I actually want.

## Arousal interaction

Precision degrades at high arousal before strength does. Why the fourth try, frustrated and pulling harder, is often less accurate than the second. [[Arousal]] and [[Attempt Discipline]].
