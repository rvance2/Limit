# Skill MOC

How much of my physical capacity reaches the rock. Lattice makes the case by omission: at V13+ finger strength explains 4% of variance and pulling strength explains none, so whatever separates those climbers lives here, in [[Recovery MOC]], or in [[Mind MOC]].

- [[Footwork]] — my largest untrained skill.
- [[Precision]] — hitting the intended target first time, hands and feet.
- [[Beta]] — sequence generation and selection. Highest return on a limit boulder.
- [[Pacing and Rhythm]] — where to move fast, where slow, where to stop.
- [[Movement Efficiency]] — measurable smoothness, and how it changes with practice.
- [[Motor Learning]] — how to structure practice so it retains.

## Measurement problem

No equivalent of a max hang. Research proxies are geometric entropy and jerk of the hip trajectory, both needing motion capture. In practice I film myself and count: attempts-to-send on a benchmark, foot slips per boulder, sequences generated per crux. Crude, works. [[Test Battery]].
