# Block 2 — Max Strength

Five weeks chasing peak force and closing the gaps between grip positions. Hardest block on my fingers.

**This block will feel bad.** Strength blocks suppress performance while I'm inside them. Boulders that went in week 6 may not go in week 10. That's the plan working, and [[Checkpoints]] separates it from the plan failing.

## Priorities in order

1. [[MED Hangs]] — minimum edge depth at bodyweight, twice or three times weekly.
2. [[Grip Specificity Block]] — **moved forward from Block 3.** Once weekly.
3. [[Recruitment Pulls]] — twice weekly, measured, maximum intent.
4. [[Limit Bouldering]] — twice weekly, [[Attempt Discipline]] enforced.
5. [[Weighted Pulls]] — 5 × 3 @ 85-90%, top double at 92% in weeks 9-11.
6. [[One Arm Progression]] — stages 4-5.
7. [[Shoulder Protocol]] and [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] — unchanged.

## Finger prescription

Weeks 7-8: `4 x MED Hangs x 8"-10" (2) :3'` twice weekly
Weeks 9-10: `4-5 x MED Hangs x 8"-10" (2) :4'` twice weekly, plus a third light session if morning stiffness is absent
Week 11: `4 x MED Hangs x 8" (1) :4'` twice weekly, margin pushed down

Edge steps down no more than once per two weeks. Within an edge, +1 s per session to the top of the range, then step.

## Why grip specificity moved here

Lattice tested 3,484 climbers: at V1-V4, open-hand testers loaded +22.5 kg against half crimp's +14.5 kg. By V12+ the two converge to within 0.2 kg. The strongest climbers have no weak positions.

Breadth is a base-phase property, not a peaking property. Building it in Block 2 rather than Block 3 leaves Block 3 free for rate work, which is where my actual gap is. [[Factors MOC]].

One or two positions per session, selected by what my project demands. Full crimp yielding sets only, before climbing, two sets. [[Grip Specificity Block]].

## Monitoring

- [[Readiness Monitoring]] every morning. Two flags → downgrade, three → skill only.
- Morning finger stiffness is the earliest tissue signal and the one I'll rationalise.

## Stop conditions for the block

Cut short and go straight to week 12 if:

- Palpation tenderness over a pulley persists more than three days.
- Morning stiffness takes over 15 minutes to clear on two consecutive mornings.
- Edge size on [[MED Hangs]] goes backwards rather than forwards.

## Week 12 — reduced **and** test week

Testing inside a reduced week is deliberate: rested numbers are comparable numbers, and the fatigue from Block 2 has cleared enough to see what it actually built.

Full [[Test Battery]]. The verdict rules are in [[Checkpoints]] — briefly, a max hang below baseline *after* the reduced week has cleared is a real regression, not fatigue masking, and Block 3's finger volume drops 30%.
