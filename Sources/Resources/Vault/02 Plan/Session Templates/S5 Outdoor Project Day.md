# S5 Outdoor Project Day

The session the other four exist for. From week 14 this replaces [[S2 Limit Boulder]] whenever conditions allow.

## Night before

1. [[Conditions Forecasting]] check: dew point forecast, rock temperature and aspect, wind. Go/no-go.
2. [[Skin Programme]] assessment. Score 1-5. Below 3, go anyway but budget fewer attempts.
3. Pack: two pairs of shoes with different compounds, brush, infrared thermometer, fan if humid, frozen bottle if hot.
4. Nine hours in bed. [[Sleep Protocol]].
5. Write the attempt budget down. Six to ten genuine. Decide now, not at the boulder.

## At the boulder

| # | Item | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm up | The walk in is not the warm-up. See below. |
| 2 | Brush the crux holds | Highest-return thing I'll do all day |
| 3 | Full warm-up: easy traverses, three easy boulders, progressive hangs on a portable edge | 25-30 min. Don't skip on a cold day |

| 7 | Log every attempt | Ten seconds each |
| 8 | Stop when the rules say stop | Not when the light goes |

## The four rules, restated because this is where they matter

1. Timer. Three minutes minimum, five on the crux. At 60 seconds I'm at ~85% of phosphagen capacity and an 85% attempt is wasted.
2. Fixed attempt budget, written down before the session.
3. Stop after three consecutive attempts worse than the day's best.
4. Log every attempt: number, highest point, genuine or rehearsal, one clause on the failure mode.

## Conditions tactics

- Shoes inside my jacket between attempts in the cold. Cold rubber stiffens and loses conformity.
- Wipe soles on a damp cloth before every real attempt.
- Fan on the crux holds between attempts if humid. It looks ridiculous.
- Brush again every three or four attempts. Old chalk is a lubricant.
- Rock temperature must be above the dew point or the hold is wet whatever my phone says about humidity.

## Approach and shins

The approach is a load event, not a warm-up. Steep, carrying a pad, is exactly the loading pattern that triggers medial tibial stress. Two weeks before a trip with real approaches, weighted walks per [[Lower Limb Resilience]]. On multi-day trips, alternate a long-approach day with a roadside day.

## Stop rules

- Three consecutive declining attempts.
- Any fingertip hot spot. Skin costs a week.
- Any shoulder sensation on a move: abandon that beta, don't repeat it.
- Conditions collapsing — dew point rising into the rock temperature. Go home. A wet hold isn't a strength problem.
