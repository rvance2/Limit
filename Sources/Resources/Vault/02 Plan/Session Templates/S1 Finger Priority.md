# S1 Finger Priority

Most important session of the week. Everything else in the week is arranged around not compromising it.

## Order

| # | Item | Time | Block variation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Universal warm-up | 20 min | — |
| 2 | [[Recruitment Pulls]] | 15 min | Blocks 2-4. 5-8 efforts, 3-5 s, max intent, 2-3 min rest |
| 3 | Primary finger method | 25 min | B1: [[Max Hangs]] MAW 20 mm · B2: [[MED Hangs]] · B3: [[Max Hangs]] + [[Grip Specificity Block]] · B4: [[Max Hangs]] 3 sets only |
| 4 | [[Limit Bouldering]] | 25 min | Blocks 2-4. 2 problems, 4-6 attempts, timer on |
| 5 | [[Weighted Pulls]] | 10 min | Blocks 1-3 |
| 6 | [[Tension Circuit]] | 15 min | All blocks |

110 minutes at the top end. Trim item 5 to make 100.

## Pre-session

[[Tendon Nutrition Timing]] 60 min before. 15 g gelatin or collagen with vitamin C.

## Block 1 variation

No [[Limit Bouldering]], no [[Recruitment Pulls]]. Item 2 becomes nothing, item 4 becomes [[Repeaters]] plus 20 minutes of moderate bouldering. Block 1 is tissue and technique, not force expression.

## Stop rules

- Grip position changing mid-hang: that set doesn't count. Two in a row ends the exercise.
- [[Recruitment Pulls]]: stop when peak drops 5% below the session best.
- Any finger pop, click, or sting: session over.
- Palpation tenderness over a pulley found during warm-up: 25-30 mm and drop load 30% for the day, then read [[Tendon Health]].
- Shoulder sensation during [[Weighted Pulls]]: exercise over for the day.

## This session's specific failure mode

Running out of time and doing the finger work last. With 60 minutes: warm-up, three sets of the primary finger method, [[Tension Circuit]], go home. A short session with the priority intact beats a full session with the priority moved to the end.
