# S6 Skill and Pull

Wednesday, Blocks 2-4. Replaces [[S4 Volume and Skill]], which stays as the Block 1 and reduced-week version.

Skill first, pull second. Skill work done after heavy pulling teaches sloppy movement, and pulling is the only thing in the week that doesn't compete neurally with fingers.

## Order

| # | Item | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Universal warm-up | 15 min |
| 2 | [[Footwork Precision]] — two drills, drawn at random | 12 min |
| 3 | [[Style Roulette]] — compressed | 25 min |
| 4 | [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] — one track, alternating | 15 min |
| 5 | [[Weighted Pulls]] — primary sets | 20 min |
| 6 | [[One Arm Progression]] — current stage, both sides | 15 min |
| 7 | Rows, 3 × 8-10 | 7 min |
| 8 | [[Hip and Ankle Mobility]] | 12 min |

121 min at the top end. Trim item 3 to 15 min and item 8 to Monday to make ~100.

Item 4 alternates tracks: underclings one session, cross-body isolations the next. It goes before the heavy pulling because it's the quality work, and after the skill work because it's loaded.

## Why Wednesday

48 hours after [[S1 Finger Priority]] and 48 before [[S3 Power and Contact]]. Pull fatigue clears in that window; putting it Monday would compromise Tuesday, and Thursday would compromise Friday.

## What this costs

[[Style Roulette]] drops from 40 to 25 minutes and [[Movement Efficiency Drills]] moves to reduced weeks and Saturday warm-ups. [[Antagonist and Wrist]] moves to Monday. Real skill hours, deliberately spent. [[Pulling Strength]] has the reasoning.

## Intensity note

Items 2 and 3 stay at or below two grades under my limit. The session is still a skill day with a strength block attached, not a hard session. If I'm trying hard on the wall here, Tuesday suffers.

## Stop rules

- Shoulder **pain** in any item: that exercise over for the day. The painless click in item 4 is expected and gets logged 0-3, not stopped.
- Click getting louder or appearing at lower loads across consecutive weeks in item 4: regress a stage.
- Asymmetry over 20% between sides in item 6: work the weak side only.
- Medial elbow tenderness: cut item 5 load 20%, double [[Antagonist and Wrist]] on Monday.
- Skin at 2/5 from Tuesday: do items 1, 4-8 and skip the wall climbing. Saturday matters more.
- Any finger stiffness on waking: items 4-8 only.
