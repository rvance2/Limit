# Week Template

Five slots. In a 4-day week, drop Wednesday and move [[Deadpoint Power]] into Monday at reduced volume.

| Day | Session | Duration | Modules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | [[S1 Finger Priority]] | 100 min | Primary finger method, [[Limit Bouldering]], [[Tension Circuit]] |
| Tue | [[S6 Skill and Pull]] (Blocks 2-4) or [[S4 Volume and Skill]] (Block 1, reduced weeks) | 100 / 90 min | [[Footwork Precision]], [[Style Roulette]], [[Weighted Pulls]], [[One Arm Progression]] |
| Wed | [[S3 Power and Contact]] | 100 min | Primary finger method (second weekly), [[Recruitment Pulls]], [[Deadpoint Power]], [[Contact Strength Ladder]] |
| Thu | Recovery | 25 min | Light [[Shoulder Protocol]] activation, [[Hip and Ankle Mobility]] |
| Fri | [[S5 Outdoor Project Day]] or [[S2 Limit Boulder]] | 3-5 h / 90 min | [[Attempt Discipline]], [[Beta Lab]] |
| Sat | Recovery | 45 min | Full [[Shoulder Protocol]], [[Deep Shoulder Positions]], [[Hip and Ankle Mobility]], [[Antagonist and Wrist]], [[Visualization Protocol]] |
| Sun | Off | — | Light [[Shoulder Protocol]] activation, review in [[Log Templates]] |

## Ordering logic

Two days between the two highest-intensity finger sessions (Mon and Fri). Neural fatigue lasts days and shows up as reduced [[Rate of Force Development]] before reduced peak force, so the gap isn't negotiable.

Recovery sits on Thursday, directly before the project day, rather than on Wednesday. A project day with nothing but a power session in front of it was the worse problem: Thursday now gives Friday a genuine buffer, and Thursday is the day that gets cut first if [[Readiness Monitoring]] flags two.

The trade: Tuesday's pull work ([[S6 Skill and Pull]]) now sits directly against Wednesday's power work with no gap, instead of 48 h clear on both sides. Pulling and catch work don't compete for the same tissue the way two finger days or two power days would, so this is the smaller cost of the two options — worth naming plainly rather than pretending both slots can be perfectly buffered with only one spare day to spend.

## Session order

Always: warm-up, then the most neurally demanding thing, then the rest.

1. Warm-up, 15-20 min
2. [[Recruitment Pulls]] if scheduled
3. Primary finger method — [[Max Hangs]] or [[MED Hangs]]
4. [[Limit Bouldering]] or [[Deadpoint Power]]
5. [[Weighted Pulls]], then [[One Arm Progression]]
6. [[Tension Circuit]], [[Lower Limb Resilience]]

Finger work never last. If the session runs long, cut from the bottom.

## Reduced weeks (6, 12, 19)

Same five slots. Changes:

- Finger volume 60%, intensity 85-90%.
- No [[Limit Bouldering]], no [[Deadpoint Power]], no [[Contact Strength Ladder]].
- Tuesday reverts to [[S4 Volume and Skill]]. [[One Arm Progression]] holds at its current stage, no progression attempts. [[Weighted Pulls]] at 60% volume.
- Wall work 2-3 grades below limit, [[Movement Efficiency Drills]] as the content.
- Everything in the Thu/Sat slots unchanged.
- When [[Skin]] heals and technique improves.

## Daily non-negotiables, any block

- [[Shoulder Protocol]]: one comprehensive session weekly (Saturday) plus light activation twice (Thursday, Sunday). [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] 2×/week.
- [[Visualization Protocol]] twice daily, two minutes.
- [[Skin Programme]] every evening.
- [[Readiness Monitoring]] every morning.
- [[Tendon Nutrition Timing]] 60 min before any finger session.
