# Block 0 — Baseline Week

One week, four sessions, nothing hard. The point is numbers I can compare against in week 12 and week 22. Without them, the next 21 weeks produce a feeling rather than a result.

## Sessions

| Day | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Anthropometry, mobility, [[Shoulder Protocol]] screen |
| Wed | Finger strength: 7-second two-arm max hang, 20 mm, half crimp |
| Fri | Pulling strength 2RM; [[Critical Force]] with a load cell |
| Sat | Benchmark boulders and video |

Protocols in [[Test Battery]]. Targets in [[Benchmarks]].

## Monday

- Bodyweight, height, arm span. Write down both ape index conventions so I stop confusing them.
- Box split heel-to-heel against a wall, toes and stomach touching, upper-body support only for entry and exit.
- Knee-to-wall ankle dorsiflexion, both sides, centimetres.
- 90/90 hip rotation range, both directions.
- Shoulder: full [[Shoulder Protocol]] at zero added resistance. Note which items I can't do with control. That list is my Block 1 priority.

## Wednesday — the important one

Lattice's protocol, exactly, so the number is comparable to their dataset:

- Warm up thoroughly. Progressive hangs, 50% to 90%.
- 20 mm edge, half crimp, both arms, thumb off.
- Start moderate. Add load each attempt. Reach maximum within four to eight attempts.
- More than eight attempts: stop, retry in 24 hours. Fatigue contaminates the number.
- Elbow angle 150-180°. No lock-off.
- Any grip position change during the hang invalidates that attempt; the last clean one is my score.
- Record total load including bodyweight, as kg and as % bodyweight.

## Friday

- Pull-up 2RM. Shoulder-width pronated, full range to chin over bar, controlled, no lower-body movement. Three minutes between attempts. Estimate 1RM from it; don't test a single.
- **Bodyweight pull-up max reps.** I get 14 on an average day and 23 on a strong one — a 9-rep spread on the same exercise is a large readiness signal and worth having a baseline for.
- **One-arm lock-off hold time**, each side, at full / 90° / 120°. This sets the entry stage for [[One Arm Progression]]. I held 10 s at full before a vacation; find out where that actually sits now.
- **[[Deep Shoulder Positions]] baseline:** heaviest two-arm undercling hold at 30 s, single-arm undercling hold time each side, cross-body adduction hold each side. Record the click score 0-3 at each.
- With a load cell: [[Critical Force]]. Rhythmic 7 s on / 3 s off all-out contractions, half crimp on 20 mm, four to five minutes, until output plateaus. Record mean end-test force and impulse above it.

## Saturday

- Three benchmark boulders: one at flash level, one at solid limit, one done once. Film all three side-on. These are the week-11 and week-22 comparison.
- Log attempts-to-send on the third.
- Count foot slips across the whole session.

## Don't

- Don't train hard this week. I want rested numbers.
- Don't skip the video. In week 22 it'll be the most convincing thing in the log, and by then I can't go back and film it.
- Don't test finger strength on a day I've already climbed hard.
