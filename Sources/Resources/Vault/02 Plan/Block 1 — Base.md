# Block 1 — Base

Five weeks building the tissue, the shoulder and the pull base that Blocks 2 and 3 spend. Shortened from six — I have training history and a tissue base, and six weeks with no limit bouldering was written for someone with less.

## Priorities in order

1. [[Shoulder Protocol]] 3×/week, building to full load with control.
2. [[Max Hangs]] with added weight on 20 mm, half crimp. One method, five weeks.
3. [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] twice weekly, both tracks from stage 1.
4. Technique: [[Footwork Precision]], [[Style Roulette]], [[Movement Efficiency Drills]].
5. [[Weighted Pulls]] base: 4 × 5 @ 75-80% est. 1RM, full range.
6. [[One Arm Progression]] re-acquisition from wherever week 0 lands — probably stage 3.
7. [[Repeaters]] for tissue tolerance, once weekly. Leaves after week 5.

## Finger prescription

Weeks 1-2: `3 x MAW Hangs x 20mm x 10" (4) :3'` twice weekly
Weeks 3-4: `4 x MAW Hangs x 20mm x 10" (3) :3'` twice weekly
Week 5: `5 x MAW Hangs x 20mm x 10"-12" (3) :4'` twice weekly

+1-2.5 kg when I hit the prescribed duration with the prescribed margin twice in a row. Ceiling 5% added load per session. [[Tendon Health]].

## RFD enters at week 4

[[Recruitment Pulls]] starts week 4 at low volume — 4 efforts, 3-5 s, twice weekly. Version one held it until week 8.

Reason: Stien et al. 2022 puts rate of force development as the measure that widens most across the band I'm crossing. It gets presence across the plan rather than one concentrated block. Four weeks of base first, then the pattern starts.

## Why no limit bouldering yet

Tissue isn't ready for the impulse loads and the pull base needs building. Block 1 buys the right to train hard in Block 2. Wall work stays two grades below limit — if I'm trying hard, I'm in the wrong block.

Five weeks is not long. The payoff is weeks 7-18 not getting interrupted.

## Starting now, running forever

[[Visualization Protocol]] twice daily · [[Skin Programme]] daily with the log · [[Readiness Monitoring]] every morning · [[Tendon Nutrition Timing]] before finger sessions · [[Lower Limb Resilience]] twice weekly · [[Antagonist and Wrist]] twice weekly · [[Hip and Ankle Mobility]] 3×/week.

## Week 6 is reduced

60% finger volume at 85-90% intensity. [[Movement Efficiency Drills]] as the wall content. When [[Skin]] recovers and technique consolidates. [[Deload]].

## Exit criteria

All of these before Block 2. See [[Checkpoints]] for what to do if one fails.

- Added weight on [[Max Hangs]] has increased across the five weeks. If flat, hold the block two more weeks.
- [[Shoulder Protocol]] at full load, 3×/week, four consecutive weeks.
- No morning finger stiffness over 15 minutes in the last two weeks, no palpation tenderness over any pulley.
- [[One Arm Progression]] back to at least the week-0 tested stage on both sides.
- [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] track A stage 3, track B stage 2, click no worse than 1/3.

Moving on from an unmet criterion is how this breaks in week 11.
