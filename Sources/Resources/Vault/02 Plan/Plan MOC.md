# Plan MOC

Twenty-two weeks, four to five sessions a week, gym bouldering plus a hangboard, outdoor projecting from week 13. No systems board, so the power block substitutes wall-based work for campusing.

## What changed, and why

Version one ran Block 1 for six weeks and Block 3 for four. Reversed, because of where I actually sit.

At V8-V10 I'm crossing Lattice's Advanced/Elite boundary, where finger strength's explanatory power roughly halves (adj R² 0.167 → 0.099). Meanwhile Stien et al. 2022 found rate of force development is what widens most into the elite band: +74.9% advanced→elite against +45.5% for the step before, while peak force climbs at a flat ~40% per band.

So:

- **Block 3 extended from four weeks to six.** RFD is the thing that most separates the band above me from mine, and it's the thing I have the least equipment for.
- **Block 1 shortened from six weeks to five.** I have training history and a tissue base. Six weeks of no limit bouldering was written for someone with less.
- **[[Grip Specificity Block]] moved from Block 3 into Block 2.** Lattice's convergence finding: the strongest climbers have no weak grip positions. Breadth is worth building before the peak phase, not during it.
- **[[Recruitment Pulls]] introduced at week 4** rather than week 8. RFD gets presence across the plan, not just a bigger block.
- **Test week moved from 11 to 12**, so it lands inside a reduced week. Rested numbers are comparable numbers.

## Why this order

Deep edge with added weight before small edges at bodyweight. López's sequencing — MAW Hangs on 18-20 mm, then MED Hangs on small edges. Small edges early in a block, before tissue has adapted, is the standard route to a pulley problem.

Force before rate. RFD is expressed force; there has to be force to develop rapidly.

Rate before performance. The campus board trial found four weekly explosive sessions built RFD while two built bouldering performance — same volume, opposite outcomes. Block 3 concentrates the rate work at two sessions a week and Block 4 converts it.

Volume down, intensity held at the end. Cut volume 40-60%, maintain intensity. Reducing intensity 30-60% cost 20-30% of performance in the reviewed studies.

## The three reduced weeks

Weeks 6, 12, 19. Every session slot stays; finger load drops to 60% at 85-90% intensity, power work disappears, and [[Shoulder Protocol]], [[Deep Shoulder Positions]], [[Hip and Ankle Mobility]], [[Footwork Precision]] and [[Visualization Protocol]] continue unchanged.

**Week 13-18 is the longest stretch between reduced weeks and it's the explosive block.** If [[Readiness Monitoring]] hits 2+ flags for three consecutive sessions, insert a light week at 16 and shorten Block 3 by one. Losing a week beats losing the block.

Arriving at a reduced week feeling great: take it anyway. [[Deload]].
