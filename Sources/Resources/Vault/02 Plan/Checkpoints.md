# Checkpoints

I don't need faith in this plan. I need to be able to check. Faith makes me follow a plan past the point where the data says change; a checkpoint tells me which is happening.

Each one names what working looks like, what not-working looks like, and what to do about it.

## The distinction that matters most

**"Working but feels bad" and "not working" produce the same sensation.** They are told apart by the numbers, never by how I feel mid-block. That's the entire reason [[Log Templates]] exists.

## Week 5 — end of [[Block 1 — Base]]

**Working:** added weight on [[Max Hangs]] has increased. Nothing else will feel different, and it isn't supposed to.

**Not working:** added weight flat across five weeks.

**Action if flat:** hold Block 1 for two more weeks rather than advancing. The exit criteria already say this. Advancing from an unmet criterion is how this breaks in week 11.

Also check: [[One Arm Progression]] back to at least the week-0 tested stage on both sides. [[Deep Shoulder Positions]] track A at stage 3, click score no worse than 1/3.

## Week 6 — first reduced week

**Working:** week 6 feels better than week 5.

**Not working:** week 6 feels the same or worse.

**Action:** feeling no better after a reduced week means Block 1 was harder than intended, not easier. Extend the reduced week to ten days and cut Block 2's opening volume 20%.

## Weeks 7–11 — [[Block 2 — Max Strength]]

**This will feel bad, and that is the plan working.** Strength blocks suppress performance while I'm inside them. I will feel weaker on the wall. Limit boulders that went in week 6 may not go in week 10.

This is the point where I will most want to abandon the plan. Write that down now so I recognise it later.

**Genuinely not working, as opposed to feeling bad:**

- Morning finger stiffness over 15 minutes on consecutive mornings
- Palpation tenderness over a pulley persisting more than three days
- Edge size on [[MED Hangs]] going backwards rather than forwards

Those are tissue signals, not fatigue signals, and they mean stop the block early and go to the reduced week. [[Tendon Health]].

## Week 12 — reduced week and test week

The first real verdict. Testing in a reduced week is deliberate: rested numbers are comparable numbers.

**Working:** 7-second max hang above the week-0 figure. [[Recruitment Pulls]] peak above the week-7 figure.

**Ambiguous:** max hang flat or slightly down. A mid-block drop is normal fatigue masking. What matters is whether it recovers by the end of the reduced week.

**Not working:** max hang still below baseline *after* the reduced week has cleared the fatigue. That's a real regression, and it means the load progression outran the tissue. Cut Block 3's finger volume 30% and extend the reduced week.

## Weeks 13–18 — [[Block 3 — Power and RFD]]

The hardest block to feel and the easiest to doubt. RFD improvements do not announce themselves. There is no sensation attached to producing force 40 milliseconds sooner.

**Working:** [[Recruitment Pulls]] session-best peak force trending up week over week. Noisy line, upward trend. That's the only signal, and it's why the module is measured every rep.

**Not working:** peak force flat across six weeks with readiness flags consistently at 0-1. That means the stimulus isn't landing — likely intent rather than programming. Two truly maximal efforts beat six nearly maximal ones. [[Intent]].

**Fatigue watch:** this is the longest stretch between reduced weeks in the plan and it's the explosive block. If [[Readiness Monitoring]] hits 2+ flags for three consecutive sessions, insert a light week at week 16 and shorten Block 3 by one. Better to lose a week than the block.

## Week 19 — final reduced week

**Working:** skin recovering toward 4-5/5 ahead of the send window. This week exists as much for skin as for fatigue. [[Skin Programme]].

**Not working:** arriving at week 20 with skin at 2/5. That means Block 3's outdoor days weren't budgeted against the [[Attempt Discipline]] rules.

## Weeks 20–22 — [[Block 4 — Peak and Send]]

**Working:** I feel strong. Volume is down 40-60%, intensity is held, and near-maximal hangs still happen.

**Not working:** I feel flat. Flat in a taper almost always means I cut intensity instead of volume — the specific mistake [[Deload]] warns about. Fix it by putting the load back on and doing fewer sets, not more.

## Week 22 — the verdict

Full [[Test Battery]] against [[Block 0 — Baseline Week]]. Compare:

| Measure | The question |
| --- | --- |
| 7 s max hang, %BW | Did peak force move? |
| [[Recruitment Pulls]] peak | Did rate move? This is the one that matters most at my level |
| Pull-up 2RM and est. 1RM | Did the pull base move? |
| One-arm lock-off, per side, 3 angles | Did the goal move? |
| Undercling load at first click | Did shoulder capacity move? |
| Benchmark boulder video, hip path | Did movement change? |
| Foot slips per session | Did precision change? |
| Attempts-to-send on benchmark 3 | Did anything transfer? |

The video comparison is the one most likely to surprise me, and it's the one I can't reconstruct later if I skip filming in week 0.

## What actually derails this

Not the programming. Three things, all boring, all mine:

1. **A pulley strain from rushing load progression.** Ceiling is 5% added weight per session and one edge step per fortnight. The rule exists because I will want to break it in week 9.
2. **Quietly abandoning the log around week 9.** Without it, week 12 and week 22 have nothing to compare against and the whole structure becomes vibes.
3. **Skipping reduced weeks because I feel good.** Feeling good in week 5 of a hard block usually means week 5 was easier than intended. [[Deload]].

## On the timescale

Twenty-two weeks is one block, not a career. Grade progression at V8-V10 is measured in years, and the research has no longitudinal data on what moves anyone between bands — see [[Factors MOC]]. A plan working perfectly still will not produce V12 by week 22.

What it can produce: measurably more force, measurably faster force, a one-arm lock-off back and extended, underclings that don't click, and a better-executed project attempt. Those are checkable. The grade follows or it doesn't.
